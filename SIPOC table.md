# SIPOC Table for Structure Inspection

## Suppliers
- Inspection Team
- Maintenance Team
- Quality Assurance Team

## Inputs
- Inspection Checklist
- Structural Blueprints
- Inspection Tools (e.g., gauges, cameras, drones)
- Previous Inspection Reports

## Process
1. Receive Inspection Request
2. Review Inspection Checklist and Blueprints
3. Conduct Physical Inspection
4. Document Findings
5. Report Issues to Maintenance Team
6. Submit Inspection Report

## Outputs
- Inspection Report
- Identified Structural Issues
- Maintenance Recommendations

## Customers
- Maintenance Team
- Quality Assurance Team
- Management

# SIPOC Table for Structure Integrity

## Suppliers
- Engineering Team
- Inspection Team
- Material Suppliers

## Inputs
- Structural Blueprints
- Material Specifications
- Integrity Assessment Tools (e.g., sensors, software)
- Previous Integrity Reports

## Process
1. Receive Integrity Assessment Request
2. Review Blueprints and Material Specifications
3. Conduct Structural Analysis
4. Evaluate Material Conditions
5. Document Findings
6. Submit Integrity Report

## Outputs
- Integrity Report
- Identified Structural Weaknesses
- Repair/Replacement Recommendations

## Customers
- Maintenance Team
- Quality Assurance Team
- Management

# Combined SIPOC Table for Structure

## Suppliers
- Inspection Team
- Engineering Team
- Maintenance Team
- Material Suppliers

## Inputs
- Inspection Checklist
- Structural Blueprints
- Inspection and Integrity Assessment Tools (e.g., gauges, cameras, drones, sensors, software)
- Material Specifications
- Previous Reports

## Process
1. Receive Inspection/Integrity Assessment Request
2. Review Inspection Checklist, Blueprints, and Material Specifications
3. Conduct Physical Inspection and Structural Analysis
4. Evaluate Material Conditions
5. Document Findings
6. Report Issues and Provide Recommendations to Maintenance Team
7. Submit Combined Inspection and Integrity Report

## Outputs
- Combined Inspection and Integrity Report
- Identified Structural Issues and Weaknesses
- Maintenance and Repair/Replacement Recommendations

## Customers
- Maintenance Team
- Quality Assurance Team
- Management

# Notes
- The combined SIPOC table allows for a streamlined process that leverages the expertise of both inspectors and engineers.
- This combined approach ensures a more comprehensive analysis of structural conditions.
- Coordination between the inspection team and engineering team is crucial for timely and accurate reporting.
